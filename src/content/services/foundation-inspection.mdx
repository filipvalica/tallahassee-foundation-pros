## Free foundation inspection in Tallahassee

The most common call we get is from a homeowner who noticed sticking doors six months ago and kept meaning to get it looked at. By the time they call, there are three new cracks in the drywall and the floor near the back bedroom has a noticeable dip.

Foundation problems in Tallahassee don't get better on their own. The soil here, a mix of sandy clay and limestone karst, shifts seasonally. Summer rains expand the clay. Fall dry periods shrink it. That cycle repeats every year, and each year the cracks get a little wider.

The inspection costs nothing. Waiting costs more than the inspection.

## What we look for

Our inspection covers the full exterior and interior of the slab and structure:

- **Exterior:** foundation perimeter for cracks, soil separation, grade slope toward the structure, and signs of water pooling near the foundation
- **Interior:** door and window alignment, floor levelness, drywall cracks at corners and door frames, baseboards pulling away from the floor, and any visible slab cracking in garage floors or utility areas
- **Drainage:** downspout discharge location, soil grade, and any visible ponding areas; drainage is the #1 root cause of preventable foundation movement in Tallahassee

We don't run ground-penetrating radar or drill inspection holes on a free inspection. What we provide is a thorough visual assessment that tells you whether the situation warrants deeper investigation, a specific repair, or nothing at all.

## What you get after the inspection

At the end of the visit, we walk through what we found in plain language. No jargon, no scare tactics. If the situation requires repair, we explain what type, why, and what it costs in writing before you agree to anything.

If you're buying or selling a home, we can produce a written foundation condition report for your file. These are commonly requested by real estate attorneys, agents, and lenders for homes built before 1985.
