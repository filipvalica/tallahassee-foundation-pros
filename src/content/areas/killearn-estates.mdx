## Foundation repair in Killearn Estates

Killearn Estates is Tallahassee's established affluent suburb, golf courses, larger lots, and mostly construction from the 1980s through early 2000s. Homeowners here are sometimes surprised when foundation issues come up. Newer construction should mean fewer problems, right?

The issue is that Killearn Estates sits on some of Tallahassee's heaviest clay soils. Red clay shrinks and expands more dramatically than sandy soils, and the larger footprint homes in Killearn have more foundation perimeter exposed to that movement. Post-2000 construction methods account for clay movement better than pre-1980 methods did, but they don't eliminate it.

The neighborhoods around the Killearn Country Club and along Shamrock Drive East see the highest concentration of calls, larger homes on premium lots where the landscaping was installed right against the foundation, creating drainage toward the structure rather than away.

## Common foundation problems in Killearn Estates

- **Drainage-driven settlement**: Killearn's larger lots mean longer drainage runs, and when the grading was done for aesthetics rather than engineering, roof water ends up near the foundation perimeter
- **Clay heave during the rainy season**: doors that stick and floor tiles that crack during June–September and then seem to improve are showing clay heave, not random movement
- **Slab cracking in garage floors**: garage slabs in Killearn's luxury homes are frequently the first sign; they're at grade and show movement before the interior does
- **Post-Hurricane Michael effects**: homes in northeast Tallahassee that experienced soil saturation during and after 2018 still show ongoing claim activity; some movement from that event is still resolving
