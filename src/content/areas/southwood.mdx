## Foundation repair in Southwood

Southwood is Tallahassee's planned upscale community in the southeast, a gated development with homes built primarily between 2000 and 2015. It's the newest large-scale residential neighborhood we service, and new construction means different problems than older Tallahassee neighborhoods.

The most common call from Southwood isn't structural failure, it's drainage. The community was developed with a consistent landscaping aesthetic, and in many cases the original landscaping installed beds and berms right against the foundation. When those drainage patterns weren't engineered to move water away from the slab, the clay-rich southeast Tallahassee soil started doing what clay does: absorbing, swelling, and shrinking.

Southwood homes are newer and built to tighter code standards than pre-1980 construction, which means the movement is usually caught earlier and the scope of repair is smaller. But "newer" doesn't mean "not at risk," and homeowners here sometimes wait longer to call because they assume newer construction can't have a foundation problem.

## Foundation issues in Southwood

- **Drainage-caused clay movement**: original landscaping against the foundation is the #1 driver in Southwood; correcting grade and extending downspouts often prevents further movement without structural repair
- **Garage floor settlement**: garage slabs are on grade and show settlement before the main slab in most cases; a dropped garage floor threshold is often the first visible sign
- **Hairline cracks at door frames**: clay expansion during the summer rainy season creates small but consistent cracks at door frame corners; these are monitoring items, not always repairs
- **Pre-purchase inspection requests**: Southwood's resale market is active; buyers requesting foundation condition reports before closing are a regular part of our Southwood workload
